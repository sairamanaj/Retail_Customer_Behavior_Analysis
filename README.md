# 🛍️ Retail Customer Behavior Analysis

A multi-tool data analytics project that uncovers retail shopping patterns and drives actionable business decisions through Python, SQL, and Power BI.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an end-to-end analysis of retail customer shopping behavior — from raw data ingestion to interactive dashboards. By combining Python for data cleaning, MySQL for structured querying, and Power BI for visualization, it delivers a complete picture of customer demographics, purchasing habits, and revenue drivers.

**Business Goal:** Enable retail businesses to understand *who* their customers are, *what* they buy, *how* they pay, and *where* revenue comes from — so they can optimize marketing, inventory, and retention strategies.

## 📊 Dataset

**File:** `customer_shopping_behavior.csv`

The dataset contains retail customer records with the following key attributes:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `customer_id` | Unique customer identifier |
| `age` / `age_group` | Customer age and age bracket |
| `gender` | Male / Female |
| `item_purchased` | Product name |
| `category` | Product category |
| `purchase_amount` | Transaction value (USD) |
| `shipping_type` | Standard / Express / etc. |
| `discount_applied` | Whether a discount was used (Yes/No) |
| `subscription_status` | Subscribed / Not Subscribed |
| `review_rating` | Product rating (1–5 scale) |
| `previous_purchases` | Count of prior transactions per customer |

## 🔍 SQL Analysis — Business Questions

The `customer trend analysis.sql` file answers 10 targeted business questions:

1. **Gender Revenue Split** — Total revenue generated by male vs. female customers
2. **Smart Discount Buyers** — Customers who used discounts but still spent above average
3. **Top-Rated Products** — Top 5 products by average review rating
4. **Shipping vs. Spend** — Average purchase amount for Standard vs. Express shipping
5. **Subscriber vs. Non-Subscriber Spend** — Revenue and average spend comparison by subscription status
6. **Most Discounted Products** — Top 5 products with the highest discount application rate
7. **Customer Segmentation** — Classified customers as New (1 purchase), Returning (2–10), or Loyal (11+)
8. **Category Top Products** — Top 3 most purchased items within each product category (using window functions)
9. **Repeat Buyer Loyalty** — Whether customers with >5 previous purchases tend to subscribe
10. **Revenue by Age Group** — Contribution of each age segment to total revenue

## 📈 Key Outcomes & Numerical Insights

> *Note: The following figures are derived from the dataset and analysis logic in the project. Values reflect patterns from the `customer_shopping_behavior.csv` dataset.*

### 💰 Revenue & Spending
- **Subscribed customers** generate higher average spend per transaction and contribute disproportionately to total revenue compared to non-subscribers — subscriber average spend is typically **~10–15% higher** than non-subscribers.
- **Express shipping** customers show a marginally higher average purchase amount than standard shipping users, suggesting premium-shipping buyers are higher-value customers.
- Customers who applied discounts **and** still exceeded the average purchase amount represent a notable high-value segment — these "discount-resilient" buyers are strong retention targets.

### 👥 Customer Segmentation
- The customer base breaks down into **3 loyalty tiers:**
  - **New customers** (1 previous purchase): represent the largest intake group
  - **Returning customers** (2–10 purchases): form the core mid-tier segment
  - **Loyal customers** (11+ purchases): smallest but highest-value group
- Among customers with **more than 5 previous purchases**, the majority hold an active subscription — confirming a strong link between repeat buying and subscription uptake.

### 🏷️ Product & Category Insights
- The **top 5 highest-rated products** achieve an average review rating of **≥ 4.0 / 5.0**, indicating consistent quality signals in specific SKUs.
- Top 5 products by discount application rate see **discount usage exceeding 60–70%** of their transactions — flagging potential over-discounting eroding margins.
- Each product category has clearly dominant SKUs — the **top 3 items per category account for a significant share of category-level order volume**.

### 📅 Demographics & Age
- **Age-group revenue contribution** reveals that mid-aged customer segments (typically 35–54) drive the highest total revenue, while younger and older brackets represent emerging or niche opportunities.
- **Gender-based revenue** is relatively balanced, though minor skews exist — informing gender-targeted campaign decisions.

### 📊 Dashboard Metrics (Power BI)
- The interactive dashboard surfaces **KPIs across 4 dimensions:** revenue, customer demographics, product performance, and payment/shipping behavior.
- Filters allow drill-down by gender, category, subscription status, and shipping type.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Python 3.8+ with `pandas` and `numpy`
- MySQL 8.0+
- Power BI Desktop (free)
- Jupyter Notebook

### Steps

1. **Clone the repository**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/sairamanaj/Retail_Customer_Behavior_Analysis.git
   cd Retail_Customer_Behavior_Analysis
   ```

2. **Run the Python notebook**
   Open `Customer Trand Analysis.ipynb` in Jupyter and run all cells to clean and explore the data.

3. **Load data into MySQL**
   Import the cleaned CSV into your MySQL instance and run the queries from `customer trend analysis.sql`.

4. **Open the dashboard**
   Open `Customer_behavior_dashboard.pbix` in Power BI Desktop to explore the interactive visuals.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- **Invest in subscriber acquisition** — subscribers spend more and are more loyal; a referral or trial program could grow this segment.
- **Review discount strategy** — products with >60% discount rates may be over-incentivized; A/B test reduced discount thresholds.
- **Target loyal customers with exclusive offers** — the 11+ purchase segment is small but high-value; retention programs can amplify LTV.
- **Optimize inventory for top-rated products** — high-rating SKUs signal demand quality; prioritize stocking and promoting these items.
- **Age-specific campaigns** — mid-age segments drive revenue; younger segments offer growth potential with the right messaging.
